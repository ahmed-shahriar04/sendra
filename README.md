## ⚡ Overview

Traditional Android file-sharing utilities have become bloated ad networks packed with intrusive trackers, forced internet permissions, and battery drain.

**Sendra** strips away the garbage and rebuilds local device-to-device transfer from the ground up:
* **Zero Advertisements:** No popups, interstitial video ads, banners, or spam notifications.
* **100% Air-Gapped Offline:** Operates entirely over direct local TCP/UDP sockets and Wi-Fi Direct/Hotspots. Data never touches any external cloud server.
* **Uncapped Hardware Speeds:** Streams large files, 4K videos, and raw folder trees at rates up to **60–100+ MB/s** over clean 5GHz bands.
* **Aesthetic Gen-Z Bento UI:** Built with custom curved surfaces, non-clipping ripples, and an OLED deep slate dark theme.
